# Fennec 1.0

Fennec watches for recurring crackling on your Mac and can repair the sound
service without closing your apps. Choose automatic repair or approve each
repair yourself.

- A live signal strip shows what Fennec detected.
- Repairs are marked as held only after the problem stays quiet; repeated
  failures make Fennec stand down.
- The helper, login item, local logs, and downloaded updates can be removed
  from the in-app uninstaller.
- Manual update checks, downloads, and installation stay under your control.
- The fox can cross the screen during a repair, with Reduce Motion respected.

Requires macOS 14.2 or later on Apple silicon. Move Fennec to `/Applications`
before enabling automatic repair.
