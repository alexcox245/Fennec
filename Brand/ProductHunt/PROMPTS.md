# Image generation prompts for the v2 Product Hunt media

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-03. The thumbnail used
`Brand/Fennec-AppIcon-Master.png` as a reference. The gallery slides used three
different illustrations from the [Fennec Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/YqkjbOVhkJV7RvC6Lk5vHP/Fennec?node-id=328-30):
walking fox `328:30`, sitting fox `326:13`, and duotone mascot `830:864`.
Reference images were not edit targets. Each output was inspected for correct
copy, pose, and mascot details.

## Gallery 1 — `gallery-01-listening-v2.png`

> Create a wide landscape Product Hunt poster from Figma node `328:30`. Keep an
> entire small cream fennec walking in profile across the orange desert, wearing
> gold aviators and black open-back headphones. Preserve the layered dunes,
> sparse shrubs, angular rocks and broad cobalt sky. Place the fox in the lower
> third with room for type above. Set exactly “Fennec” and “Watchfox for
> crackling Mac audio” in large cream native sans serif. No rounded corners,
> fake UI, other characters or other text.

## Gallery 2 — `gallery-02-signal-v2.png`

> Create a wide landscape Product Hunt poster from Figma node `326:13`. Keep the
> entire cream fox seated upright in the orange desert, with gold aviators,
> black headphones, rock piles, shrubs and open blue sky. Place the seated fox
> in the lower-left third, with exactly “The crackle lingers.” and “Fennec spots
> the failure signal.” in large cream type in the upper-right sky. A tiny pair
> of orange crackle marks may sit by a distant rock. No fake UI or other text.
> Extend sky and desert to all four canvas corners for a full-bleed rectangle.

## Gallery 3 — `gallery-03-repair-v2.png`

> Create a visually distinct wide duotone poster from Figma node `830:864`.
> Use a warm cream background and an enormous cropped orange fennec-head motif
> on the right, with a blue headphone arch and blue aviator lenses. Leave the
> left half for exact text: “Your call.”, “Ask first or choose automatic repair.”,
> and “Your apps stay open.” Bold ink native sans serif, safe margins. No rocks,
> landscape, fake controls, decorative effects or other text.

## Thumbnail — `thumbnail-v2.png`

> Create a square Product Hunt thumbnail matching the master app icon. Center a
> symmetrical, unsmiling cream fennec head with huge ears, gold-rimmed dark
> aviators, and black open-back mesh headphones with cords. Use a cobalt sky
> and orange dune ridge. Crop tightly and simplify details for readability at
> 48 pixels. Full color reaches all four edges. No text, border, gradient,
> texture, glow, added objects or watermark.

Across the set, the intended colors were sampled from the master art:
`#2373C3` sky, `#EF792D` dune, `#BD5D26` dune shadow, `#FDDCAF` cream,
`#2C2927` ink, and `#DA963E` aviator gold. These specifications apply only to
the launch graphics; changing the shipped app palette remains T-009.
